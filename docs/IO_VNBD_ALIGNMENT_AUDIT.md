# IO-VNBD synchronized-pair alignment audit

Generated by `python scripts/audit_iovnbd_pairs.py` from the synchronized categorized portion of the downloaded IO-VNBD archive.

## First-pass result

- 72 phone logs found; all 72 had a same-directory vehicle CSV partner.
- 72 pairs were parsed successfully.
- 65/72 pass the provisional clock gate: p95 absolute phone/vehicle timestamp-offset residual no greater than 50 ms, with no phone timestamp parse failures.
- 63/72 pairs have equal phone/vehicle row counts. Equal row counts are not required when timestamp interpolation is used.
- Seven pairs require manual review before labels are built: `M`, `S3b`, `S4`, `Vta17`, `Vta29`, `Vtb1`, and `Y1`.

S1 passes with an estimated phone-minus-vehicle offset of 3,600.545 s and a p95 residual of 1 ms. Offsets vary by drive because phone and vehicle clocks use different time origins/time zones; estimate each pair's offset from its own timestamps.

## Initial drive-level split proposal

For a first model comparison, use complete drives as the unit of splitting:

- **Train:** 41 passing Driver E drives from `Vf`, `Vta`, and `Vtb` (40 were usable after one non-increasing timestamp run was skipped during window preparation).
- **Validation:** 20 passing Driver E drives from `Vw`, a separate drive family.
- **Test:** 4 passing Driver A drives (`S1`, `S2`, `S3a`, `S3c`), held out from training.
- **Review/exclude for now:** the seven failed drives listed above.

This gives a held-out driver/device-group check, but the public synchronized subset only has two driver groups passing this clock gate. The first dataset build used 64 drives after additionally skipping `Vtb2` for non-increasing timestamps. Treat the split as preliminary generalization evidence, and revisit it if manual review recovers additional drives.

## Interpretation and training gate

This is a timestamp consistency screen, not proof that every sensor channel is physically synchronized or clean. The seven failed pairs may reflect clock drift, logger behavior, or malformed sections; inspect them before excluding them permanently. For passing drives, interpolate vehicle speed onto phone timestamp rows and keep all vehicle fields out of the phone model's input features.

Start with session-level splits, keeping complete drives separate. Reserve a held-out driver/session group where possible to check whether the model generalizes beyond a route or phone placement. Do not randomly split samples from a drive. Report results on the held-out set and retain the list of excluded or reviewed drives in the benchmark output.

The machine-readable full table is generated at `outputs/iovnbd_pair_audit.csv`; the raw dataset and outputs remain ignored by Git.
